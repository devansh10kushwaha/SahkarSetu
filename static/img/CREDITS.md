# Image Credits

Worker photographs in `static/img/` (m01–m34, f01–f26, w1, w2) are sourced from the
**FairFace dataset** (Indian-only subset), originally published by:

> Kim, E., Grogan, S., & Larson, M. (2021). *Image-based Gender and Age Classification*
> — FairFace dataset, https://github.com/dchen236/FairFace (license: CC BY 4.0).

Mirror used: https://huggingface.co/datasets/piadev/fairface-indian

Photos were filtered (adult-only, color, sharpness) and manually curated for this demo.
All names, businesses, bookings, reviews and other data in SahkarSetu are synthetic.
Photos depict real individuals from a public research dataset; for any production use,
replace with consented worker photos and delete this demo asset set.
